📌 Strategic Insights & EDA Report Conclusions

1. The Loyalty Penalty Gap

Monthly Income correlates strongly with Total Working Years (0.77), but noticeably weaker with Years at Company (0.51). Internal wage growth lags external market rates, incentivizing senior staff to jump companies to secure market pay.

2. Class Imbalance Impact on ML Modeling

With only 16.1% attrition, standard accuracy metrics will be misleading. Future predictive classification models must evaluate Precision, Recall, PR-AUC, and F1-score.

3. Compensation & Sales Role Interventions

Sales staff and lower-income cohorts (<$5,000/mo) represent 70%+ of total departures. HR leadership must conduct internal pay equity audits and restructure sales incentives.
