📌 Strategic Insights & Notebook Preprocessing Findings

1. Median Imputation on Manager Tenure

57 missing values in YearsWithCurrManager were resolved using median imputation (3.0 years), preserving nearly 4% of the dataset without introducing extreme TENURE bias.

2. IQR Compensation Outlier Capping

Executive salaries up to $19,999 were capped at the upper IQR bound ($16,583.50), stabilizing model variance while maintaining rank order.

3. Overtime & Sales Department Vulnerability

Overtime employees experience a 30.5% attrition rate compared to ~10% for non-overtime staff. Sales Executives & Lab Techs show the highest absolute turnover count.
